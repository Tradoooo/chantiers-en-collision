Chantiers en collision

Jira knows who is working on which code, people and agents alike. Every work in progress has a footprint on the repository; every overlap is flagged before the first line is written — and the app says what to do: wait, coordinate, split, or run in parallel safely.

The problem

One person starts four coding agents in parallel. Worktrees isolate files, not intentions. In twenty minutes, four worksites have diverged: two migrations carry the same number, a renamed service breaks three callers, the lockfile was regenerated twice. The conflict is discovered at merge time and resolved by an agent that never knew the other's intent.

The verdict

Every declaration answers in under two seconds: FREE CAUTION WAIT STOP — always with the neighbour's name, the cited path and the course of action. No anonymous warnings.

Nothing eternal

Reservations expire (2 h default, 24 h max). Idle worksites go dormant, then close themselves. The hook fails open: if the app doesn't answer in three seconds, the agent keeps working. Hard mode only denies where the project decided.

The live demo world

Worksite map · zones · who touches

The repository tree coloured by occupation (spec §12): who is where, since when, with which overlap degree. The zone catalog gives each part of the repo its nature — exclusive, hotspot, frozen or out of scope — and the parallel width tells how many agents this repo supports at once.

Sprint planning · waves · dispatcher

plan-parallel-work turns the sprint into collision-free waves, hinge tickets first and alone. The sprint check lists the risky pairs, and the dispatcher answers "the next clear ticket for this worker".

Worksite panel · collisions · notices · reservations

The ticket panel: footprint by source (predicted, declared, observed, attested), neighbours, collisions with their six outcomes — wait, sequence, coordinate, split, merge, override — the worksite mailbox and bounded reservations.

Operations · hook · CI · lifecycle · prediction · learning

What happens without the app's cooperation: the hook observes every write (and denies in hard mode), the CI attests the branch footprint at push time, idle worksites go dormant then close, reservations expire — and the app learns: prediction accuracy per component, zone reclassification proposals, the four numbers.

Collision degrees

DegreeSituationVerdict
D0Same named zone, different filesfree — neighbour mentioned
D1Same foldercaution
D2Same filecaution — read the neighbour first
D3Same file, deleted / moved / renamed by one sidewait or coordinate
D4Exclusive resource, reserved or frozen zonewait — stop in hard mode
D5Broken contract (lot C)coordinate

Hotspots lower the degree one step; so does a dormant neighbour; a prediction-only footprint is capped at D1 and labelled “probable”.
